Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 July 2026

This policy explains what information the You Zodiac app collects, how it's used, and the choices you have. You Zodiac is operated by an Australian sole trader (“we,” “us”).

1. The short version

You Zodiac doesn't require an account, doesn't run advertising, and doesn't sell your data. The birth details you enter — yours and, optionally, a partner's — are stored only on your own device. We never see or store them on our own servers.

2. Information you provide

To generate your chart and readings, the app asks for:

  • Your date of birth, and — if you choose to add them — birth time and birth place
  • Optionally, the same details for a partner, to generate a compatibility reading

This information is stored locally on your device using standard on-device storage. It is not uploaded to a You Zodiac account or database, because You Zodiac doesn't have one — there is no sign-up or login.

3. How chart calculations work

Calculating a birth chart or a daily reading requires astronomical and astrological calculation that happens outside the app. When you enter birth details, the app sends the date, time, and place of birth to a third-party astrological calculation service solely to compute chart positions and aspects, and to a geocoding service to resolve place names to coordinates. These providers process this data to return the calculation results to the app; we do not instruct them to retain it beyond what's needed to fulfil that request, and You Zodiac itself does not store this data on any server we operate.

4. Data you control

Because everything is stored on your device, you're always in control of it:

  • You can clear your saved chart data at any time from Settings → Data
  • Deleting the app removes all locally stored information with it
  • There is no cloud account to delete, because none is created

5. Subscriptions and payment

You Zodiac may offer optional paid subscriptions for additional features. Where this applies, payment is handled entirely by the Apple App Store, and subscription status is managed through our subscription provider, RevenueCat. We do not receive or store your payment card details — Apple and RevenueCat process this on our behalf, governed by their own privacy policies.

6. What we don't do

  • We don't require you to create an account
  • We don't run third-party advertising in the app
  • We don't sell or rent your personal information to anyone

7. Children's privacy

You Zodiac is not directed at children, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact us using the details below and we'll address it.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app changes — for example, if we introduce optional analytics or new features that affect how data is handled. We'll update the "last updated" date above when we do. Significant changes will be reflected here before they take effect.

9. Contact

Questions about this policy or your data can be sent to admin@youzodiac.com.

This policy is provided as a general description of how You Zodiac handles data and is not a substitute for tailored legal advice. Before publishing, we recommend having it reviewed by a qualified professional against the Australian Privacy Act and Apple's current App Store requirements, particularly once subscriptions or analytics are added.
